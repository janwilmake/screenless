how it works

Three steps, then you just call.

1

Install

One command installs the CLI and verifies your phone number.

2

Arm a session

Type /screenless in Claude Code. That session now answers the line.

3

Call

Ring the number, or call it on WhatsApp. A phone call hangs up and rings you straight back, so a spoofed caller id gets nowhere. Ask, decide, have it push or deploy: answers take seconds to a few minutes, and it tells you how it is going in between.

your team

One number for the whole team.

Everyone calls the same line and reaches their own session. Around that, a few things a team gets for free.

Teammates can listen in

Share your session's brief with your team, and a teammate who rings hears where it stands. What they ask is noted for after the call; it never runs on your machine while they are on the line.

Nobody watching? It waits.

Call while no session is armed and your request is transcribed and queued for the next teammate's terminal that watches, for up to a week.

Your agents can call you

Skills built on the line: call-when-afk phones you when an agent is blocked, and morning-pr-review rings with last night's pull requests.

faq

The things you were about to ask.

What can it do during a call?

Anything your session can do at the keyboard: read, edit, run tests, commit, push, deploy, post to Slack. It will not read a secret out loud.

What if someone spoofs my number?

They get nowhere. The line never answers a phone call on its caller id: it hangs up and calls your verified number back, so only your phone gets through. A WhatsApp call is tied to your account.

Does my code leave my machine?

No. Your session reads and changes it locally. What passes through us is the call itself: its audio, the transcript, and the short answers your session sends back to be spoken.

Do I have to speak English?

No. It follows whatever language you speak, and switches when you do.

Can I read the install script first?

Please do. It is plain bash, right here, and the CLI has no dependencies. All of it is on GitHub, MIT.

What does cancelling look like?

There is nothing to cancel: pay as you go, no subscription. Stop calling and you stop spending.
