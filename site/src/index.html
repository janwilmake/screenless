your rules

Your session, your machine, your say.

You decide what each person gets

Hears where things stand, gets answers, or gets things done. Set per person on your team page, changed any time, and every change logged.

Only your team gets through

A phone call is never answered on its caller id: the line rings the caller's verified number back. A WhatsApp call is tied to their account.

Your code stays on your machine

Your session does the work, with the access it already has. The voice on the phone holds no keys; what passes through us is the call itself.

in meetings

Bring it to the meeting.

Send your session into a Google Meet, now or at the meeting's start. It joins under its own name, says in the chat that it is an AI that keeps a transcript, and stays quiet until someone says its name. Then it answers out loud, from the meeting itself or from your code, tickets and docs.

Half an hour before, your session prepares: who is coming, what is still open, the questions likely to come up, answered in advance. You set what the room may have it do. Afterwards the whole meeting, with every speaker named, goes back to your session.

faq

The things you were about to ask.

Doesn't Claude Code already do this?

It lets you reach your agent. This lets your team reach it: by phone, with no laptop, seat or account, under rules you set. The quick questions stop coming to you.

Why this, and not Slack?

Use both: Slack is great at a desk. screenless is for the rest: a call on the road or between meetings, or a WhatsApp message with a screenshot from wherever they are. And it is not a fresh bot: it reaches the session you are working in, which knows the branch you are on, the change you have not committed yet, and why yesterday's deploy was rolled back.

Can they message instead of calling?

Yes, on WhatsApp, to the same number. Text, screenshots and voice notes go to your session, which answers in the chat. A conversation keeps its context, so a follow-up just works, until it has been quiet for half an hour.

Can it join our meetings?

Yes, Google Meet today; Zoom and Teams links work but are untested. Your session sends it with screenless meet <link>, or accepts the invite and joins at the start. Someone in the meeting lets it in. You give the room one level: only listen, look things up, or get things done. It speaks English in meetings for now.

What if my laptop is closed?

Then nobody gets a live answer, because the session runs on your machine. The call is still taken: what they ask is transcribed and waits, up to a week, for your session or a teammate's. For answers around the clock, run it on a machine that stays on, or keep your laptop awake: the guide.

What exactly can a teammate do?

What you give them, per person on your team page: hear where things stand (what they ask is handled after the call), get answers from your code, tickets and docs, or get things done (your session files the ticket, sends the message, ships the fix). In the CLI these are listen, read and full, and a .agents/permissions.md in your repo can define levels and rules of your own, with no names in it.

What if someone spoofs a number?

They get nowhere. The line never answers a phone call on its caller id: it hangs up and calls the verified number back, so only that person's phone gets through. A WhatsApp call is tied to the caller's account.

Does my code leave my machine?

No. Your session reads and changes it locally. What passes through us is the call itself: its audio, the transcript, and the short answers your session sends back to be spoken.

Do callers have to speak English?

No. It follows whatever language they speak, and switches when they do.

Can I read the code first?

Please do. It is open source, MIT, on GitHub; the install script is plain bash, and the CLI has no dependencies. To install by hand instead: curl -fsSL https://screenless.sh/install | bash

What does cancelling look like?

Cancel the plan on your team's billing page; it runs to the end of the month you paid for. The two-week trial needs no card, so there is nothing to cancel there.
